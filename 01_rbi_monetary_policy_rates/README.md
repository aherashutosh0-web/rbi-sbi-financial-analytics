# P01 — RBI Monetary Policy & Reserve Requirements Tracker

> **Domain:** Macro-Finance · Monetary Policy  ·  **Accent colour:** `BLUE` (#2D4E8F) — see portfolio
> `COLOR_THEORY.md`

## Business Question
How have RBI's policy rates and reserve requirements evolved, and what is the current monetary stance for banking operations?

## Data Sources (real, downloaded)
Reserve Bank of India — *Handbook of Statistics on Indian Economy* (2025-26). Real XLSX tables downloaded from `rbidocs.rbi.org.in` (HBS table numbers cited in `data_lake/rbi_hbs_index.json`).

| Dataset | Grain | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| `policy_rates` | RBI HBS Table **40** | annual | 107 |
