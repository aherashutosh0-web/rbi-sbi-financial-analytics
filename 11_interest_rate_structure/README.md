# P11 — Interest Rate Structure & Transmission Analysis

> **Domain:** Banking Sector · Rates  ·  **Accent colour:** `VIOLET` (#7E57C2) — see portfolio
> `COLOR_THEORY.md`

## Business Question
How are deposit and lending rates distributed across maturities and borrower classes, and how complete is policy transmission?

## Data Sources (real, downloaded)
Reserve Bank of India — *Handbook of Statistics on Indian Economy* (2025-26). Real XLSX tables downloaded from `rbidocs.rbi.org.in` (HBS table numbers cited in `data_lake/rbi_hbs_index.json`).

| Dataset | Grain | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| `interest_structure` | RBI HBS Table **59** | annual | 18 |
| `policy_rates` | RBI HBS Table **40** | annual | 107 |
